Support

Something not working, or a question about your home? Email n.irembaz@gmail.com. Tell us what you were trying to do and, if you can, add a screenshot. We usually reply within two working days.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. You can make a chore plan without one. Create an account when you want to share your home with someone.

How do I invite my partner or roommate?

Go to Home → Invite someone and share the invitation. They install Fridge Club, create an account and choose I have an invite. Fridge Club is available on iPhone only for now.

Does Fridge Club send money?

No. Fridge Club records who paid for what and who owes whom. When you pay someone back by cash or bank transfer, record the repayment in the app to update the balance.

Can other people see my private room?

No. Rooms marked as private, and their chores and history, are visible only to you.

I didn’t get the email code.

Check your spam folder, wait a minute, then ask for a new code. Each code works for one hour.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot your password?. We will email you a code to set a new one. This signs you out on your other devices.

How do I leave a home?

Go to Home → Home & account settings → Leave this home. Settle your balance and hand over your chores first. If you own the home, make someone else the owner before you leave.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Home, tap the account settings row and choose Delete account. Confirm with your password. You don’t need to settle your balance first: it stays on the record so everyone else’s balance stays the same.

If you can’t open the app, email us from the address on your account with the subject “Delete my account” and we will delete it within 30 days. What happens to your data is explained in our Privacy Policy.
